- Ukraine's Unmanned Systems Forces struck a Russian S-400 air defense position near Gelendzhik, Krasnodar region, overnight between August 8 and 9, 2026.
- Commander Robert "Madyar" Brovdi said drones also destroyed four other Russian air defense elements and damaged three Black Sea shadow fleet vessels in the same operation.
A Russian air defense battery that had been launching missiles at Ukrainian cities for days finally went up in flames, and Ukraine’s drone operators are taking credit for putting it there.
Ukraine’s Unmanned Systems Forces, a dedicated branch of the country’s military built specifically around drone warfare, struck a Russian S-400 Triumf air defense position near the Black Sea resort town of Gelendzhik in Russia’s Krasnodar region, according to Ukrainska Pravda, which cited a report from the branch’s commander.
The S-400 Triumf is one of Russia’s most advanced long-range air defense systems, a mobile network of radars and missile launchers designed to detect and shoot down aircraft, cruise missiles and even some ballistic missiles from hundreds of kilometers away. Losing even a single battery matters because these systems form the backbone of Russia’s air defense umbrella, and each one removed from the map creates an opening for Ukrainian aircraft, drones or missiles to strike deeper into Russian territory with less risk of being intercepted along the way.
According to the report, Ukraine’s Unmanned Systems Forces said the targeted S-400 position had carried out six missile launches against Ukraine the day before the strike, a detail that framed the operation as a direct response to an active threat rather than a strike on a dormant target. Robert “Madyar” Brovdi, the officer who commands Ukraine’s Unmanned Systems Forces and has become one of the most visible faces of the country’s drone war effort, said the position burned for roughly three hours after being hit, according to Ukrainian outlet UNN, which cited his public statement, with the site located on the shoreline directly beside a set of coastal catacombs used for military purposes.
The strike on Gelendzhik was not an isolated action but part of a broader overnight operation that Ukraine’s Unmanned Systems Forces described hitting multiple targets across Russian-held territory and the Black Sea. Brovdi said drone forces destroyed four additional Russian air defense elements overnight between August 8 and 9, all located inside Russia, according to UNN‘s reporting, suggesting a coordinated push against Russian air defense infrastructure rather than a single opportunistic strike. The same operation also reportedly damaged three vessels belonging to what Western officials and Ukrainian intelligence commonly refer to as Russia’s shadow fleet, a collection of aging oil tankers and cargo ships that operate under flags of convenience and murky ownership structures specifically to help Russia sell oil abroad while dodging Western sanctions imposed after its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022. Brovdi specified the three vessels struck in the Black Sea included one tanker and two dry cargo ships, according to the UNN report.
Gelendzhik itself sits along Russia’s Black Sea coast near Novorossiysk, a major port city that handles a significant share of Russia’s oil exports and has increasingly become a target for Ukrainian long-range drone strikes as Kyiv works to squeeze the revenue streams funding Moscow’s war effort. The area has seen repeated drone activity in recent days, with strikes also reported against Novorossiysk and the nearby port of Tuapse in the same window, according to Ukrainian outlet Espreso, reflecting a sustained campaign rather than a single night’s action. Russian authorities have not issued an official, detailed public accounting of the Gelendzhik strike or confirmed the extent of damage to the S-400 position, and independent verification of battlefield claims from either side of the war remains difficult given the restrictions both Ukraine and Russia place on reporting from active strike zones.
Ukraine’s Unmanned Systems Forces, formally established as its own branch in 2024, has grown into one of the most closely watched elements of Kyiv’s military, built around the idea that inexpensive, mass-produced drones can inflict outsized damage on expensive, hard-to-replace Russian systems like the S-400, whose individual batteries reportedly cost well over $100 million to produce and field.

Gen Z was 'misled,' says Dharmendra Pradhan
Former Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan broke his silence for the first time since he resigned following massive youth protests.
Speaking at a ceremony on Saturday, Pradhan said: "Gen Z is our children. Some people attempted to mislead them."
Pradhan's acknowledgment of the protests and his resignation came some 15 days after he quit as the education minister.
Pradhan said he "suffered" because of Gen Z's stubbornness but had come to realize that the new generation must be understood rather than resisted.
Speaking about his resignation, Pradhan said he had approached Prime Minister Narendra Modi and offered to resign from the Union Council of Ministers.
"The post was not important to me," Pradhan told a gathering at an event at GM University in Bhubaneswar, according to Indian news agency Press Trust of India.
Pradhan's resignation was the biggest demand made by the young protesters, led by the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP).
The protests were triggered over exam irregularities in competitive exams, especially the NEET exam, which is a key requirement for those wishing to enter medical schools across India.
Reportedly, at least 21 students had taken their own lives after the NEET exam was cancelled because of a question paper leak. It was then held at a later date.

BELGOROD, August 9. /TASS/. Three civilians were killed and 25 others, including children aged four and nine, were injured in an overnight Ukrainian drone attack on Belgorod, Acting Governor Alexander Shuvaev said on Max.
"Last night, Belgorod once again came under a massive terrorist drone attack by the neo-Nazi Kiev regime. Regrettably, according to updated information, three civilians were killed. I offer my sincere condolences to their families. I understand that no words can ease this pain for their relatives and loved ones. We all share your grief and mourn with you," he wrote.
One of the injured children has been hospitalized in moderate condition. Thirteen injured adults remain in hospitals, with one in critical condition, Shuvaev said. "All necessary assistance is being provided. I wish all those injured a speedy recovery," he wrote.
The attack damaged numerous residential buildings, administrative facilities, and social and commercial sites. Fires broke out in parked vehicles, two apartment buildings and a private house, but firefighters extinguished all of them, the acting governor said.
Russia’s Investigative Committee has opened a criminal case on a terrorist attack following the Ukrainian strikes on Belgorod, committee spokeswoman Svetlana Petrenko told TASS.
